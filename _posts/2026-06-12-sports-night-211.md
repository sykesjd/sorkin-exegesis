I feel like I've harped on it too much, but at the same time it bears repeating how the half-hour format for _Sports Night_ serves to cause its tonal inconsistencies to stick out like a sore thumb in a way tonal changes don't in _The West Wing_. This episode provides examples of that in spades, with a couple of serious storylines rather superficially tapped in the service of a massive dose of comedy. That isn't the say the comedy isn't great, mind you — but it does nonetheless feel arguably out of place for an otherwise serialized series. I'm sure you'll see what I mean as we now step through the episode.
