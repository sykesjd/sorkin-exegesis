One of the criticisms Aaron Sorkin received for his work on _The Newsroom_ was its veritable overdose in hindsight, with its near-past setting serving to relitigate recent events from the eye of a future observer. Perhaps lost in those criticisms is how Mr. Sorkin had some practice in the practice of hindsight over a decade earlier with early episodes of _The West Wing_. Where other episodes are relatively subtle about it ("Five Votes Down", for one), this episode seriously hits the viewer over the head with the hindsight — which is quite fascinating considering how many former Clinton administration officials were and would be consultants on the show.

Much like _The Newsroom_, of course, much of that hindsight ended up being for naught in the real world — but we'll get there when we get there. For now:

> MANDY: _(V.O.)_ Previously on _The West Wing_...

Let's step through this episode.

> BARTLET: With nearly 18 million new jobs, wages rising at more than twice the rate of inflation, the highest home ownership in history, the smallest welfare rolls in thirty years, and the lowest peacetime unemployment since 1957, I stand before you to report that America has created the longest peacetime economic expansion in our history.

I did a cross-check with President Clinton's 1996 State of the Union (you'll see why I chose that one in particular shortly) to do a compare-and-contrast on the language herein, because it sounds quite realistic. On the economy, the 1996 SotU boasts eight million new jobs rather than 18 million, with "over a million of them in basic industries, like construction and automobiles", and also reports "the lowest combined rates of unemployment and inflation in 27 years". The speech is also quick to point out how both welfare and food stamp rolls are down, though it doesn't provide a number like we hear here, instead lumping its lowness in with the crime, poverty, and teen pregnancy rates. As for the "peacetime economic expansion" part... well, the 1996 SotU starts off with a thanks for American troops in Bosnia. There's our indication this is a parallel universe, I guess.

Let's be real for a moment: a lot happens and happens quickly in this episode.
