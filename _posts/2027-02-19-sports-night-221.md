For those who had to look it up like I did: John Rocker was at the time a relief pitcher for the Atlanta Braves who a little over five months prior to this episode made disparaging comments about New York City when asked whether he'd accept an offer from the Yankees or the Mets. If that sounds familiar to you, you're probably remembering the episode "Shane" from earlier in this season — but it should be noted that episode aired twenty days prior to Rocker's comments' being published. If you then think Rocker was inspired by that episode, I would urge you to reconsider considering the massive volume of slurs and xenophobia present in his comments. Dan and Casey are right to call him a doofus — frankly, stronger words are warranted, but you can't say them on television.
