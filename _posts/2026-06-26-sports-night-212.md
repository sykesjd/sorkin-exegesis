Yeah, okay, I really don't have much positive to say about this episode. Casey's off-screen moment of awesome is basically the high point, while everything else is essentially wasted time. The return of Sam, which should have been a moment of charge for the season, ends up falling considerably flat, especially when coupled with Isaac's mentally checking out from the whole situation. I'm almost tempted to wonder whether the entire point of this episode was an underhanded infomercial for the recently-released Jordan cologne, but somehow I don't think Mr. Sorkin is that hard of a sell-out. Whatever the case, this episode of _Sports Night_ is almost certainly the most easily skippable.
