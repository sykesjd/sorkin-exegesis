"UPDATED FINAL", reads the draft script — god fucking damn it.

And once again, updates were _still_ made after this draft — though to be fair, it was more along the lines of "this is too long" rather than "this needs to change". Still doesn't excuse using the word 'final' so quixotically...

Mr. Sorkin's return to the writing credits certainly comes for an opportune story beat, but this episode still has its patches of doesn't-age-well that I'm unfortunately not completely inclined to blame on the co-writer. Those patches aside, however, the episode speaks well for itself — which we'll see in action as we now step through the episode.
