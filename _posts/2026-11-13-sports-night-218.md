Now, I should be upfront: this is definitely not a bad episode of television — far from it. In point of fact, the continued friction between Dan and Casey makes for a well-done exploration into what has otherwise been a rather rosy relationship up until recently. That Jeremy's relationship with Jenny came to a sudden end simply contributes to the whiplash of plot thread indecision throughout this season of _Sports Night_, as well-written as that story was in this episode as well. To be fair, it's quite easy to say that plot thread had worn out its welcome, but the pattern of plot threads' wearing out their welcome leaves me weary for something to stick around for a whole season. The introduction of Dana's latent nervousness frankly doesn't do anything for me, so I don't expect that to be a major plot point going forward, and so we are left simply with the Dan and Casey friction. How much longer will that last? Viewers probably didn't have any idea at the time, but the track record of this season so far suggests not long.
