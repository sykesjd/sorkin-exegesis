So, what should I say about this episode? On the front of existing plot threads continued herein, we have a few fronts to mention. The continuing adventures of Natalie's post-breakup psychosis reaches a level of pettiness that's honestly grating to watch; the thread of Isaac's struggles with keeping his hold on reality in the wake of his stroke, while providing a perfect opportunity to repurpose some unused material, feels rather lightly touched on in the end; the tension between Casey and Dan rather underhandedly introduced eleven episodes earlier similarly gets a rather underhanded treatment in this episode as well. In the wake of those weaknesses, as well as the music of punctuation and repetition exhibited outside those threads, we therefore have to conclude that this episode essentially aims to set our expectations for future episodes to focus primarily on Jeremy's budding relationship with a sex worker.
